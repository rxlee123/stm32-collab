# Rules that shape the design

Summary of the UTM Robocon **Autocar Challenge** game rules. The official rule book is the reference.

**Robot**
- At most 20 × 20 × 20 cm before the start; no limit after the start.
- Only the Blue Pill and the Blue Bee may be commercial boards (rule 4.4). Everything else is your own circuit.
- Exactly **one** servo that moves a mechanism (rule 4.5).
- Stage 2 power supply at most 12 V.

**Stage 1: line following (3 minutes)**
- Line 20 mm wide. Checkpoints A–F scored in order, one-way arrows, dead ends.
- Where the line is white on a black background, follow the white line (7.3.8).
- Move the 5 cm cube from the Cube Zone into Box A (2 points) or Box B (1 point) before the Finish Zone; push, drag or carry.
- Stop completely inside the Finish Zone (2 points); first to finish +1.
- Start by pressing a button on the robot after the judge's signal.
- Retry: hand up, "RETRY!", restart from the last checkpoint reached. No code changes.

**Stage 2: manual match (3 minutes)**
- Push ping pong balls (40 mm) up your ramp into the opponent's area.
- Move within 15 s of the start or be disqualified. Never touch the opponent's area.
- "Dangerous servo movement" is a violation in both stages.
